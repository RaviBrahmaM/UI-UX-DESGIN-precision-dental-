Design Rationale

Target Users
This design is mainly created for local users who are looking for services and want a quick and simple way to access them. Most users prefer fast information and easy contact options without any confusion.

UX Strategy
The overall layout is designed to make things easy for the user. Each section guides the user step-by-step, helping them quickly understand the service and take action without wasting time.

Key Decisions
* Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons are placed in visible areas so users can easily take action
* Navigation is kept simple so users don’t get confused
* The design is mobile-friendly, considering that many users access websites on their phones

Conversion Focus
* The number of steps required to contact is reduced
* Service information is presented clearly and simply
* A quick and easy contact form is provided to encourage user interaction
